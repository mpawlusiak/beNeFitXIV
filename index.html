About Neurofibromatosis (NF)

Neurofibromatosis (NF) causes tumors to grow on nerves throughout the body and may lead to deafness, blindness, learning disabilities, disfigurement, disabling pain, and cancer. It affects one in every 2,500 people.

NFX

Established by Dan and Jennifer Gilbert to fund groundbreaking NF research in the areas of vision restoration, gene therapy, and more.

Children's Tumor Foundation

Drives research, expands knowledge, and advances care for the NF community.
